About Us

Welcome to Step Brews, where every cup is a step toward a flavorful journey! As part of Chilli Garlic Oil (CGO), Step Brews is more than just a coffee shop; it’s a fusion of passion, community, and the love for great coffee. Nestled in the vibrant heart of Lapu-Lapu City, Step Brews invites you to take a sip and experience the warmth that our coffee brings.

The Step Brew Experience

Crafted with Care:

Step Brews is proud to be a part of the CGO family, and just like our renowned Chilli Garlic Oil, our commitment to quality is unwavering. From the carefully selected coffee beans to the artful preparation by our skilled baristas, each cup at Step Brews is a testament to our dedication to excellence.

Local Flavor, Global Inspiration:

Inspired by the rich cultural tapestry of Lapu-Lapu City, Step Brews offers a menu that celebrates local flavors while embracing global coffee traditions. Whether you crave a bold espresso, a velvety latte, or an adventurous specialty brew, Step Brews is your destination for a diverse and satisfying coffee experience.

Community Hub:

As part of CGO, we are committed to sustainability. Our coffee beans are sourced responsibly, and we actively support local farmers. Join us in savoring the richness of our coffee while contributing to a more sustainable and eco-friendly future.
